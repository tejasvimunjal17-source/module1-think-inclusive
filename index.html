Walk in Their Shoes

Eight hands-on stations to feel, spot and fix learning barriers, and to see the strengths of different minds.

Simulations, a matching game, myth-busting, an interactive story with voices, a barrier-fixing puzzle, riddles, a personal strengths map and a self-check rubric that ends in a badge.

An important note. These activities build understanding and empathy. They are not diagnostic tests. Only qualified professionals can identify autism, ADHD, dyslexia or other conditions, and teachers should never label a student. Our job is to remove barriers so every learner can succeed, with or without a diagnosis.
1

Feel the Barrier ✓ done

Three short simulations. Try each in “Barrier mode”, answer the question, then switch on “Support mode” and feel the difference.

These simulations give a small taste of some experiences. They are approximations: every person’s experience is different, and many neurodivergent people also have real strengths in these areas.

2

Who’s in My Class? ✓ done

Read each learner’s story and match it to the profile it describes best. Each correct match reveals strengths and one simple support.

Matched: 0 of 6

Remember: a profile is not a box. Two autistic learners, or two learners with ADHD, can be very different. Many people have more than one profile.

3

Myth or Fact? ✓ done

Ten statements you may have heard in staff rooms. Decide, then read why.

4

A Day in Zoya’s Online Class ✓ done

An interactive story. You are Zoya’s teacher. Make three decisions and see how her day changes. Press 🔊 to hear each scene.

5

Fix the Barrier ✓ done

Six common features of digital courses. For each one, pick the fix that helps the most learners, without needing anyone to disclose a diagnosis.

Barriers fixed: 0 of 6

6

Who Am I? Concept Riddles ✓ done

Each riddle describes a key idea from Module 1. Think first, then reveal.

7

My Spiky Profile ✓ done

Rate yourself from 1 to 10 on each area. This is not a test: it is a mirror. Watch your shape appear, and compare it with the “average” circle.

8

Self-Check Rubric and Badge ✓ done

Write a short reflection (in the forum or your journal), then rate yourself honestly on each criterion. Click one box per row.

Reflection prompt: “Which station changed my thinking the most, what barrier will I remove from my own course, and how?” (150–250 words, or a 2-minute audio or video)
